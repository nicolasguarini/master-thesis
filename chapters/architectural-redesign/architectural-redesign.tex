\chapter{Architectural Redesign}\label{ch:architectural-redesign}

Given the numerous critical issues identified in the analysis of the current system in Chapter \ref{ch:as-is}, it is clear that a comprehensive redesign is required. The new design must perform the system's current functions more efficiently and in an optimized manner, while also addressing a set of requirements that did not exist at the time of the system's original development and that the current system is unable to support, as it was not designed for them.
